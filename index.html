Changelog

LATEST

Update 0.35 Beta - QOL and Convenience 2

  • Added configurable auto-stop on rare drops with a special reveal animation.
  • Added a Buy Max button for the Luck upgrade.
  • Undiscovered rarities now show as ??? in the Collection until unlocked.
  • Moved rarity details into a click-to-open Collection info panel.
  • Revamped the Settings page with categorized sections and descriptions.

Update 0.3 Beta - Name Change

  • Renamed the game to RNG Roller and replaced Coins with Money ($).
  • Removed the 40% auto roll penalty so auto rolls award full money.
  • Increased auto roll cost to $1,000 and sped up the base interval to 0.90s.
  • Added a roll reveal animation and a setting to turn it on or off.
  • Expanded the Collection to 10 rarities and boosted higher-tier odds.
  • Revamped Statistics with highlight cards and rarity distribution bars.

Update 0.2 Beta - Improving

  • Auto roll now applies and consumes Focus boosts.
  • Luck gains 0.1× per upgrade with no level cap.
  • Coins and Luck now appear below navigation.
  • Simplified collection odds.
  • Replaced Coin Bonus with Auto Speed.
  • Added On/Off settings buttons.
  • Refreshed the design and separated project files.

Update 0.15 Beta - Convenience

  • Removed the manual roll cooldown.
  • Reduced auto roll cost to 250 coins.
  • Simplified upgrade names and added Settings.

Update 0.1 Beta - RNG 101

  • Introduced rolls, rarities, coins, and upgrades.
  • Added Focus, auto roll, collection, and statistics.
